Autonomous Vehicle Motion Planning via Recurrent Spline Optimization

创建于 2024-10-09 · 最后修改于 2025-03-15 · cant

$ \begin{gathered} \dot{x}=v\cos\theta\\ \dot{y}=v\sin\theta\\ \dot{\theta}=v\tan\frac{\delta}{L}\\ \dot{\delta}=\omega \end{gathered} $

自行车模型微分平坦到x,y及其对s的各阶导数

$ \begin{gathered} v=\sqrt{\dot{x}^2+\dot{y}^2}\\ \theta=\arctan{\frac{\dot{y}}{\dot{x}}}\\ \delta=\arctan{\frac{(\ddot{y}\dot{x}-\ddot{x}\dot{y})L}{(\dot{x}^2+\dot{y}^2)^{3/2}}} \end{gathered} $

用s三次样条表达x、y

$ \begin{gathered} f_i(s)=a_{i0}+a_{i1}(s-s_i)+a_{i2}(s-s_i)^2+a_{i3}(s-s_i)^3\\ g_i(s)=b_{i0}+b_{i1}(s-s_i)+b_{i2}(s-s_i)^2+b_{i3}(s-s_i)^3 \end{gathered} $

目标函数是最小化二阶和三阶导数

$ \min_{a_{ij},b_{ij}}\sum_{i=0}^Nw_1(\ddot{f_i}(s_i)^2+\ddot{g_i}(s_i)^2)+w_2(\dddot{f_i}(s_i)^2+\dddot{g_i}(s_i)^2) $

x，y关于弧长的导数的物理意义并非Minimum Energy，用微分平坦的话Minimum Energy应该是下x,y关于时间的导数才对。没有直接的物理意义，但是就等价的理解成关于时间的导数，然后这里差了一个速度v或者v^2吧，这表达和apollo轨迹规划的物理含义一样没有直接意义

根据对$ \theta $的微分平坦可以写出对$ \theta $的硬约束

$ \theta=\arctan(\frac{\dot{y}}{\dot{x}})\rightarrow $每段起始点的heading约束$ -\dot{f_i}(s_i)\sin(\theta_i)+\dot{g_i}(s_i)\cos(\theta_i)=0 $,$ \theta_i $为对应的heading硬约束

曲率同理

$ \kappa=-\frac{\dot{y}}{(\dot{x}^2+\dot{y}^2)^{3/2}}\ddot{x}+\frac{\dot{x}}{(\dot{x}^2+\dot{y}^2)^{3/2}}\ddot{y}=\lambda\ddot{x}+\mu \ddot{y} $

此表达式为非线性需要在每个迭代点处将$ \lambda,\mu $近似计算出来

$ d\sigma=\sqrt{dx^2+dy^2} $为优化后的弧微分

$ \frac{d\sigma}{ds}=\sqrt{(\frac{dx}{ds})^2+(\frac{dy}{ds})^2}=\sqrt{\dot{x}^2+\dot{y}^2} $

当优化前后的spline弧长近似不变时$ \frac{d\sigma}{ds}\approx 1 $则可将下式近似为

$ \begin{gathered} \lambda=\frac{-1}{\dot{x}^2+\dot{y}^2}\frac{\dot{y}}{\sqrt{\dot{x}^2+\dot{y}^2}}\approx -sin(\theta_{ref})\\ \mu=\frac{-1}{\dot{x}^2+\dot{y}^2}\frac{\dot{x}}{\sqrt{\dot{x}^2+\dot{y}^2}}\approx cos(\theta_{ref})\\ \end{gathered} $

在第一次进行优化化或初始点时使用$ \kappa_i\approx -\ddot{x}_isin(\theta_{ref_i})+\ddot{y}_icos(\theta_{ref_i}) $作为近似曲率约束进行优化

在后续的迭代优化种将上次结果的$ \dot{x},\dot{y} $结果求出并带入$ \lambda,\mu $求解中(做了二次导小量假设)

$ \begin{gathered} \kappa_{lower}\leq\lambda_i\ddot{x}_i+\mu_i\ddot{y}_i\leq\kappa_{upper}\\ \kappa_{init} =-\ddot{x}_0 sin(\theta_{ref_0})+\ddot{y}_0 cos(\theta_{ref_0}) \end{gathered} $

同时需要更新投影边界

将问题描述为最优控制问题，以下一段起点为上一段末尾的形式写为，以x为例子

$ \begin{gathered} x_{i+1}=f_i(s_{i+1})=a_{i0}+a_{i1}h+a_{i2}h^2+a_{i3}h^3\\ \dot{x}_{i+1}=\dot{f}_i(s_{i+1})=a_{i1}+2a_{i2}h+3a_{i3}h^2\\ \ddot{x}_{i+1}=\ddot{f}_i(s_{i+1})=2a_{i2}+6a_{i3}h\\ \dddot{x}_{i+1}=\dddot{f}_i(s_{i+1})=6a_{i3}\\ h=s_{i+1}-s_{i} \end{gathered} $

矩阵表达为

$ \begin{bmatrix} x_{i+1} \\ \dot{x}_{i+1} \\ \ddot{x}_{i+1}\\ \dddot{x}_{i+1} \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & h & h^2 & h^3\\ 0 & 1 & h & h^2 \\ 0 & 0 & 2 & 6h\\ 0 & 0 & 0 & 6 \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} a_{i0} \\ a_{i1} \\ a_{i2}\\ a_{i3} \end{bmatrix} $

$ \begin{gathered} x_i=f_i(s_i)=a_{i0},\dot{x}_i=\dot{f}_i(s_i)=a_{i1}\\ \ddot{x}_i=\ddot{f}_i(s_i)=2a_{i2},\dddot{x}_i=\dddot{f}_i(s_i)=6a_{i3} \end{gathered} $

x’‘‘作为u,{x,x’,x’’’}作为X

$ X_{i+1}=AX_i+Bu_i\\ A=\begin{bmatrix} 1 & h & \frac{1}{2}h^2 \\ 0 & 1 & h \\ 0 & 0 & 1 \end{bmatrix} B=\begin{bmatrix} \frac{1}{6}h^3 \\ \frac{1}{2}h^2 \\ h \end{bmatrix} $

综合二维写成

{x0’’’,y0’’’,..xN’’’,yN’’’}作为u,{x0,x0’,x0’’,y0,y0’,y0’’,…,xN,xN’,xN’’,yN,yN’,yN’’}作为X

$ \hat{X}_{i+1}=\hat{A}\hat{X}_i+\hat{B}\hat{u}_i\\ \hat{A}=\begin{bmatrix} A & \\ & A \\ \end{bmatrix} \hat{B}=\begin{bmatrix} B & \\ & B \\ \end{bmatrix} $

类似qpspline的box约束

朝向为$ \theta_{ref} $的box约束，优化后位置不会超出这个box。注意x,y多项式，所处坐标系为which spline起点为坐标原点的坐标系。(方向与地图坐标系一致)

$ \begin{gathered} l_{lower}\leq-(x_i-x_{iref})\sin\theta_{ref}+(y_i-y_{iref})\cos\theta_{ref}\leq l_{upper}\\ s_{lower}\leq(x_i-x_{iref})\cos\theta_{ref}+(y_i-y_{iref})\sin\theta_{ref}\leq s_{upper} \end{gathered} $

可以给上述box约束加横纵向的松弛

除了上述约束外还有初态和终态约束

$ \begin{gathered} x_0=x_{init},y_0=y_{init}\\ x_N=x_{goal},y_N=y_{goal} \end{gathered} $
