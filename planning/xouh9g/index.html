线性化以及最优控制求解

创建于 2022-09-14 · 最后修改于 2025-03-03 · cant

线性化车体方程

X = (x,y,yaw,steer) u = dsteer

对时间的导数

$ x'=V_{const}cos(yaw)=f_1 $

$ y'=V_{const}sin(yaw)=f_2 $

$ yaw'=w=tan(steer)*V_{const}/L=f_3 $

$ steer'=dsteer=f_4 $

$ X'=\begin{bmatrix} f_1\\ f_2\\ f_3\\ f_4\\ \end{bmatrix}=f(X,u) $

将系统在上个工作点展开线性化，希望得到下面这种线性方程的形式

$ x'(t)=Ax(t)+Bu(t)+c $

下面将系统方程在这个点上线性化$ (x_{last},y_{last},yaw_{last},steer_{last}) $

$ x'\approx x'(x_{last},y_{last},yaw_{last},steer_{last})+\frac{dx'}{dX}|_{X_{last},u_{last}}(X-X_{last})+\frac{dx'}{du}|_{X_{last},u_{last}}(u-u_{last}) $

因为x只与Vconst和yaw有关所以可以简化为下式

$ x'\approx x'(yaw_{last})+\frac{dx'}{dyaw}|_{yaw_{last}}(yaw-yaw_{last})=V_{const}cos(yaw_{last})-V_{cosnt}sin(yaw_{last})(yaw-yaw_{last}) $

同理得到如下

$ y'\approx y'(yaw_{last})+\frac{dy'}{dyaw}|_{yaw_{last}}(yaw-yaw_{last})=V_{const}sin(yaw_{last})+V_{cosnt}cos(yaw_{last})(yaw-yaw_{last}) $

$ yaw'\approx yaw'(steer_{last})+\frac{dyaw'}{dsteer}|_{steer_{last}}(steer-steer_{last})=tan(steer_{last})*V_{const}/L+\frac{V_{const}}{cos^2(steer_{last})L}(steer-steer_{last}) $

$ \begin{gathered} steer'=steer'_{last}+\frac{dsteer'}{dX}|_{X_{last},u_{last}}(X-X_{last})+\frac{dsteer'}{d dsteer}|_{X_{last},dsteer_{last}}(dsteer-dsteer_{last})\\ =steer'_{last}+1*(dsteer-dsteer_{last})=dsteer_{last}+dsteer-dsteer_{last}=dsteer \end{gathered} $

得到系统方程如下,没有写成误差方程的形式，误差全部都写到c向量当中了

$ \begin{bmatrix} x'\\ y'\\ yaw'\\ steer'\\ \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} x'_{last}\\ y'_{last}\\ yaw'_{last}\\ steer'_{last}\\ \end{bmatrix}+ \begin{bmatrix} \frac{\partial f_1}{\partial x}& \frac{\partial f_1}{\partial y}& \frac{\partial f_1}{\partial yaw}& \frac{\partial f_1}{\partial steer}\\ \frac{\partial f_2}{\partial x}& \frac{\partial f_2}{\partial y}& \frac{\partial f_2}{\partial yaw}& \frac{\partial f_2}{\partial steer} \\ \frac{\partial f_3}{\partial x}& \frac{\partial f_3}{\partial y}& \frac{\partial f_3}{\partial yaw}& \frac{\partial f_3}{\partial steer}\\ \frac{\partial f_4}{\partial x}& \frac{\partial f_4}{\partial y}& \frac{\partial f_4}{\partial yaw}& \frac{\partial f_4}{\partial steer}\ \end{bmatrix}_{X_{last},U_{last}} \begin{bmatrix} x-x_{last}\\ y-y_{last}\\ yaw-yaw_{last}\\ steer-steer_{last}\\ \end{bmatrix} + \begin{bmatrix} \frac{\partial f_1}{\partial dsteer}\\ \frac{\partial f_2}{\partial dsteer}\\ \frac{\partial f_3}{\partial dsteer}\\ \frac{\partial f_4}{\partial dsteer}\\ \end{bmatrix}_{X_{last},U_{last}} (dsteer-dsteer_{last}) $

$ \begin{bmatrix} x'\\ y'\\ yaw'\\ steer'\\ \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} x'_{last}\\ y'_{last}\\ yaw'_{last}\\ steer'_{last}\\ \end{bmatrix}+ \begin{bmatrix} 0& 0& -V_{const}sin(yaw_{last})& 0\\ 0& 0& V_{const}cos(yaw_{last})& 0\\ 0& 0& 0& \frac{V_{const}}{cos^2(steer_{last})L} \\ 0& 0& 0& 0\\ \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} x-x_{last}\\ y-y_{last}\\ yaw-yaw_{last}\\ steer-steer_{last}\\ \end{bmatrix} + \begin{bmatrix} 0\\ 0\\ 0\\ 1\\ \end{bmatrix} (dsteer-dsteer_{last}) $

$ \begin{bmatrix} x'\\ y'\\ yaw'\\ steer'\\ \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} 0& 0& -V_{const}sin(yaw_{last})& 0\\ 0& 0& V_{const}cos(yaw_{last})& 0\\ 0& 0& 0& \frac{V_{const}}{cos^2(steer_{last})L} \\ 0& 0& 0& 0\\ \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} x\\ y\\ yaw\\ steer\\ \end{bmatrix} + \begin{bmatrix} 0\\ 0\\ 0\\ 1\\ \end{bmatrix} dsteer + \begin{bmatrix} V_{const}cos(yaw_{last})\\ V_{const}sin(yaw_{last})\\ tan(steer_{last})*V_{const}/L\\ dsteer_{last}\\ \end{bmatrix} + \begin{bmatrix} yaw_{last}V_{const}sin(yaw_{last})\\ -yaw_{last}V_{const}cos(yaw_{last})\\ -steer_{last}\frac{V_{const}}{cos^2(steer_{last})L}\\ -dsteer_{last}\\ \end{bmatrix} $

与下面得到的结果一致

$ x'=Ax+Bu+c $

$ \begin{bmatrix} x'\\ y'\\ yaw'\\ steer'\\ \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} 0& 0& -V_{const}sin(yaw_{last})& 0\\ 0& 0& V_{const}cos(yaw_{last})& 0\\ 0& 0& 0& \frac{V_{const}}{cos^2(steer_{last})L} \\ 0& 0& 0& 0\\ \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} x\\ y\\ yaw\\ steer\\ \end{bmatrix} + \begin{bmatrix} 0\\ 0\\ 0\\ 1\\ \end{bmatrix} dsteer + \begin{bmatrix} V_{const}cos(yaw_{last})+V_{const}yaw_{last}sin(yaw_{last})\\ V_{const}sin(yaw_{last})-V_{const}yaw_{last}cos(yaw_{last})\\ tan(steer_{last})*V_{const}/L-\frac{V_{const}}{cos^2(steer_{last})L}steer_{last}\\ 0\\ \end{bmatrix} $

前向欧拉离散化系统方程

$ x'=Ax+Bu+c=\frac{x_{k+1}-x_k}{T}=Ax_k+Bu_k+c $

移项得到如下

$ x_{k+1}=(I+TA)x_k+TBu_k+Tc $

$ \begin{bmatrix} x_{k+1}\\ y_{k+1}\\ yaw_{k+1}\\ steer_{k+1}\\ \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} 1& 0& -V_{const}Tsin(yaw_{last})& 0\\ 0& 1& V_{const}Tcos(yaw_{last})& 0\\ 0& 0& 1& \frac{V_{const}T}{cos^2(steer_{last})L} \\ 0& 0& 0& 1\\ \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} x_{k}\\ y_{k}\\ yaw_{k}\\ steer_{k}\\ \end{bmatrix} + \begin{bmatrix} 0\\ 0\\ 0\\ T\\ \end{bmatrix} dsteer_k + \begin{bmatrix} V_{const}Tcos(yaw_{last})+V_{const}Tyaw_{last}sin(yaw_{last})\\ V_{const}Tsin(yaw_{last})-V_{const}Tyaw_{last}cos(yaw_{last})\\ tan(steer_{last})*V_{const}T/L-\frac{V_{const}T}{cos^2(steer_{last})L}steer_{last}\\ 0\\ \end{bmatrix} $
