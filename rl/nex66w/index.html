12 TRPO 4 最终问题的描述形式

创建于 2022-02-06 · 最后修改于 2022-09-06 · cant

$ M_i(\pi) = L_{\pi_i}(\pi)-C\underset{s}{max}D_{KL}(\pi_i(\cdot|s)||\pi(\cdot|s)) $

待优化函数$ \underset{\pi_{i+1}}{max} L_{\pi_i}(\pi_{i+1})-C\underset{s}{max}D_{KL}(\pi_i(\cdot|s)||\pi_{i+1}(\cdot|s)) $

可以将上面的式子写成待优化函数和约束条件的形式

L-CD的表达式非常复杂 直接优化是很复杂的，所以用了近似的方法 首先用了拉格朗日乘数法的原理 使得最优化问题转换为下式有约束条件的优化。δ理论上是与C有关的常数，但没有办法精确求解所以只能人为设定。

$ \underset{\pi_{\theta}}{max} L_{\pi_{\theta_{old}}}(\pi_{\theta})s.t.\,\underset{s}{max}D_{KL}(\pi_{\theta_{old}}(\cdot|s)||\pi_{\theta}(\cdot|s)) \leqslant \delta $

对KL散度的最大值做约束，这实际上是对所有状态的KL散度做约束$ C=\frac{4\gamma\varepsilon}{(1-\gamma)^{2}} $这样约束条件就是无穷多个 无法求解，所以实际实现中将这个约束条件放松变为对平均KL散度做约束。

$ \underset{\pi_{\theta}}{max} L_{\pi_{\theta_{old}}}(\pi_{\theta})s.t.\,D_{KL}^{\rho_{\theta_{old}}}(\pi_{\theta_{old}}||\pi_{\theta}) \lt \delta $

将L展开

$ \begin{gathered} \underset{\theta}{max}L_{\pi_{\theta_{old}}}(\pi_{\theta})=\underset{\theta}{max}\,\eta(\pi_{\theta_{old}})+E_{s\sim\pi_{\theta_{old}},a\sim\pi_{\theta}}[\sum_{t=0}^{\infty}\gamma ^{t}A_{\pi_{\theta_{old}}}(s_{t},a_{t})]\\=\underset{\theta}{max}\,\eta(\pi_{\theta_{old}})+\sum_{s}\rho_{\pi_{\theta_{old}}}(s)\sum_{a}\pi_{\theta}(a|s)A_{\pi_{\theta_{old}}}(s,a)]\\\eta(\pi_{\theta_{old}})\,is\,constant\therefore\\=\underset{\theta}{max}\,\sum_{s}\rho_{\pi_{\theta_{old}}}(s)\sum_{a}\pi_{\theta}(a|s)A_{\pi_{\theta_{old}}}(s,a)]\\s.t.\,D_{KL}^{\rho_{\pi_{\theta_{old}}}}(\pi_{\theta_{old}}||\pi_{\theta})\leqslant \delta \end{gathered} $ $ D_{KL}^{\rho_{\pi_{\theta_{old}}}}(\pi_{\theta_{old}}||\pi_{\theta})=E_{s\sim \rho_{\theta_{old}}}[D_{KL}(\pi_{\theta_{old}}(\cdot|s)||\pi_{\theta}(\cdot|s))] $因为限制了KL散度所以我们可以认为$ P_{\pi^{'}}(s_t) $与$ P_{\pi}(s_t) $相等，使用important sampling 后

$ \begin{gathered} \sum_{a}\pi_{\theta}(a|s_i)A_{\pi_{\theta_{old}}}(s_i,a)]=E_{a\sim\pi_{\theta}}[A_{\pi_{\theta_{old}}}(s_i,a)]\\=E_{a\sim\pi_{\theta_{old}}}[\frac{P_{\pi_\theta}(s_i,a)}{P_{\pi_{\theta_{old}}}(s_i,a)}]A_{\pi_{\theta_{old}}}(s_i,a)]\\=E_{a\sim\pi_{\theta_{old}}}[\frac{P_{\pi_\theta}(a|s_i)P_{\pi_{\theta}}(s_i)}{P_{\pi_{\theta_{old}}}(a|s_i)P_{\pi_{\theta_{old}}}(s_i)}]A_{\pi_{\theta_{old}}}(s_i,a)] \\=E_{a\sim\pi_{\theta_{old}}}[\frac{P_{\pi_\theta}(a|s_i)}{P_{\pi_{\theta_{old}}}(a|s_i)}]A_{\pi_{\theta_{old}}}(s_i,a)]\\=E_{a\sim\pi_{\theta_{old}}}[\frac{\pi_\theta(a|s_i)}{\pi_{\theta_{old}}(a|s_i)}]A_{\pi_{\theta_{old}}}(s_i,a)] \end{gathered} $

得到了用于训练的式子

$ \begin{gathered} \underset{\theta}{max}\,\sum_{s}\rho_{\pi_{\theta_{old}}}(s)\sum_{a}\pi_{\theta}(a|s)A_{\pi_{\theta_{old}}}(s,a)\\=\underset{\theta}{max}\,\sum_{s}\rho_{\pi_{\theta_{old}}}(s)\cdot\pi_{\theta_{old}}(a|s)\sum_{a}\frac{\pi_{\theta}(a|s)}{\pi_{\theta_{old}}(a|s)}A_{\pi_{\theta_{old}}}(s,a)\\=\underset{\theta}{max}\,\sum_{s}\rho_{\pi_{\theta_{old}}}(s,a)\sum_{a}\frac{\pi_{\theta}(a|s)}{\pi_{\theta_{old}}(a|s)}A_{\pi_{\theta_{old}}}(s,a)\\=E_{s\sim\rho_{\pi_{\theta_{old}}},a\sim\pi_{\theta_{old}}}[\frac{\pi_{\theta}(a|s)}{\pi_{\theta_{old}}(a|s)}A_{\pi_{\theta_{old}}}(s,a)]\\s.t.\,E_{s\sim \rho_{\theta_{old}}}[D_{KL}(\pi_{\theta_{old}}(\cdot|s)||\pi_{\theta}(\cdot|s))]\leqslant \delta \end{gathered} $

$ \rho_{\pi_{\theta_{old}}} $是在老策略下遇到各种状态的折扣概率

将待优化函数中的动作这一层期望展开

$ \begin{gathered} \sum_{s}\rho_{\pi_{\theta_{old}}}(s)\sum_{a}\pi_{\theta}(a|s)A_{\pi_{\theta_{old}}}(s,a)\\=\sum_{s}\rho_{\pi_{\theta_{old}}}(s)\sum_{a}\pi_{\theta}(a|s)[Q_{\pi_{\theta_{old}}}(s,a)-V_{\pi_{\theta_{old}}}(s)]\\=\sum_{s}\rho_{\pi_{\theta_{old}}}(s)\sum_{a}\pi_{\theta}(a|s)Q_{\pi_{\theta_{old}}}(s,a)-\sum_{s}\rho_{\pi_{\theta_{old}}}(s)V_{\pi_{\theta_{old}}}(s)\sum_{a}\pi_{\theta}(a|s)\\=\sum_{s}\rho_{\pi_{\theta_{old}}}(s)\sum_{a}\pi_{\theta}(a|s)Q_{\pi_{\theta_{old}}}(s,a)-\sum_{s}\rho_{\pi_{\theta_{old}}}(s)V_{\pi_{\theta_{old}}}(s)\cdot 1 \end{gathered} $

可以看出优势项中的V与要求导的参数θ无关

所以可以将待优化函数改写为

$ \begin{gathered} \underset{\theta}{max}\sum_{s}\rho_{\pi_{\theta_{old}}}(s)\sum_{a}\pi_{\theta}(a|s)Q_{\pi_{\theta_{old}}}(s,a)\\=E_{s\sim\rho_{\pi_{\theta_{old}}},a\sim\pi_{\theta_{old}}}[\frac{\pi_{\theta}(a|s)}{\pi_{\theta_{old}}(a|s)}Q_{\pi_{\theta_{old}}}(s,a)]\\s.t.\,E_{s\sim \rho_{\theta_{old}}}[D_{KL}(\pi_{\theta_{old}}(\cdot|s)||\pi_{\theta}(\cdot|s))]\leqslant \delta \end{gathered} $

TRPO在实现的时候也是用到AC形式

实际计算

我们对待优化函数 L做一阶泰勒展开 对约束项KL散度做二阶泰勒展开(就像natural PG一样)，其实在讲natural PG时都讲过了

将L在θ_old 一阶泰勒展开

其中

$ L_{\theta_{old}}(\theta)\approx \eta(\pi_{\theta_{old}}) + (\triangledown_{\theta} L_{\theta_{old}}(\theta)|_{\theta=\theta_{old}})^T\cdot(\theta - \theta_{old});L_{\theta_{old}}(\theta_{old})=\eta(\pi_{\theta_{old}}) $其中n(θ_old)为定值 不需要优化所以我们的实际优化项为

$ (\triangledown_{\theta} L_{\theta_{old}}(\theta)|_{\theta=\theta_{old}})^T\cdot(\theta - \theta_{old}) $

在自然梯度那里我们对KL做过二阶展开，已经证明过

$ \begin{gathered} D_{KL}^{\rho_{\pi_{\theta_{old}}}}(\pi_{\theta_{old}}||\pi_{\theta})=E_{s\sim \rho_{\theta_{old}}}[D_{KL}(\pi_{\theta_{old}}(\cdot|s)||\pi_{\theta}(\cdot|s))] \\ \approx (\theta - \theta_{old})^T F (\theta - \theta_{old})/2\\F=\triangledown_{\theta}^2D_{KL}^{\rho_{\pi_{\theta_{old}}}}(\pi_{\theta_{old}}||\pi_{\theta})|_{\theta=\theta_{old}} \end{gathered} $

F为KL散度对参数θ的二次导数(海森阵)在θ_old处的值，也就是费舍尔信息阵(NATURAL pg有证过)

优化问题变为

$ \begin{gathered} \underset{\theta}{max}(\triangledown_{\theta} L_{\theta_{old}}(\theta)|_{\theta=\theta_{old}})^T\cdot(\theta - \theta_{old})\\s.t.(\theta - \theta_{old})^T F (\theta - \theta_{old})/2 \leqslant \delta \end{gathered} $只有等式约束才能改写为拉格朗日形式？

$ \underset{\theta}{max}(\triangledown_{\theta} L_{\theta_{old}}(\theta)|_{\theta=\theta_{old}})^T\cdot(\theta - \theta_{old})-\lambda((\theta - \theta_{old})^T F (\theta - \theta_{old})/2 - \delta) $展开是为了将函数变为一 xtAx+xtb+c的纯凸函数形式有唯一最小值，并且此行式可以通过共轭梯度快速求最优优化方向

更新参数

$ \widetilde{\triangledown_{\theta}}L(\theta) = F^{-1}\triangledown_{\theta}L(\theta) $

$ lr = \frac{1}{\lambda}=\sqrt{\frac{2\lambda}{\widetilde{\triangledown_{\theta}}L(\theta)^TF\widetilde{\triangledown_{\theta}}L(\theta)}}=\sqrt{\frac{2\lambda}{\triangledown_{\theta}L(\theta)^T(F^{-1})^T\triangledown_{\theta}L(\theta)}} $

$ \theta = \theta_{old} - lr\cdot F^{-1}\triangledown_{\theta}L(\theta) $

但是F阵和F-1的计算量都极大 这里使用共轭梯度的方法计算。
